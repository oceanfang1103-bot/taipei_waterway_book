This document records editorial choices in the English edition. The Chinese edition is retained as the source of record.

## Items for subject-matter review

1. **Dataset interpretation:** Confirm whether the 3,022 locations represent modeled susceptibility polygons, recorded flood events, or another dataset. The English edition describes them as centroids derived from susceptibility polygons, following Chapter 3.
2. **Density measure:** Point count divided by distance-band width is not conventional areal density. The English edition therefore calls it “width-standardized density” and does not describe it as points per square kilometre.
3. **Fourfold-density statement:** The Chinese Chapter 4 states that density within buffers exceeds the citywide average by more than four times, but the areas used to calculate that ratio are not shown. The English edition flags the need for inside/outside land-area values rather than presenting the ratio as established.
4. **Historical map title:** Confirm the preferred scholarly English title for 《臺灣堡圖》. This edition uses *Taiwan Fortress Map* provisionally and retains the Chinese title.
5. **Canal romanization:** Confirm the institutionally preferred romanization for 瑠公圳, 霧裡薛圳, and 大坪林圳. This edition uses Liugong, Wulixue, and Dapinglin.
6. **Palimpsest attribution:** Meinig and Sauer support layered readings of landscape, but the precise attribution of “landscape as palimpsest” should be checked against the cited passages before formal academic publication.
7. **Statistical wording:** With 1,000 simulations and no simulated statistic as extreme as the observation, report whether the intended Monte Carlo value is $p < 0.001$ or the finite-sample estimate $(0+1)/(1000+1)$.

## Editorial principles

- Proper nouns remain identifiable in both English and Chinese.
- Claims are translated conservatively; “supports” is preferred to “proves” where causal identification is not established.
- The English prose follows academic English syntax rather than Chinese word order.
- Quantities and reported results are unchanged except where the wording clarifies the measure being reported.
